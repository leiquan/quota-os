Next native port dependency
---------------------------
WTF::OSAllocator (Source/WTF/wtf/OSAllocator.h in that WebKit commit):
reserve / commit / decommit, guard pages, and executable pages.
OSAllocatorPOSIX.cpp implements those with mmap and mprotect.
A Quota OS allocator would have to refuse executable mappings and
guard pages while the MMU is off, and take its bytes from a contiguous
arena carved out before Tier-1 coloring, only for -append webkit, so
the 128M admission figures above stay put. That allocator is not in
this commit. Without it, JavaScriptCore and WebCore are not linkable.
WebKitCommon.cmake turns WebCore off when JavaScriptCore is off, and
turns WebKit off when WebCore is off. ALL_PORTS has no QuotaOS port.

(this kernel is -ffreestanding C), bmalloc, ICU, libxml2, sqlite,
harfbuzz, libpng, libjpeg, libwebp, then WebCore's GraphicsContext
aimed at the existing XRGB8888 surface, and PlatformKeyboardEvent
fed by the virtio EV_KEY events. The WPE 2.53.3 options file also
requires glib, libsoup, and epoxy. None of that is built here.
